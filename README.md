# SHEETAL

**Sustainable Hydrogel-enabled Energy Efficient Thermal Augmentation for Air-conditioning Load reduction**

Team **MEGHDOOT** — School of Energy Science and Engineering, IIT Kharagpur
Submitted to the **Platinum Jubilee Innovation Challenge 2026** (IIT Kharagpur)

**[→ View the project website](https://vinayarya29.github.io/sheetal/)**

A passive, retrofit-friendly PAM-LiCl hydrogel panel that clips onto the air intake of a split
air-conditioner's outdoor condenser unit. The hygroscopic hydrogel absorbs atmospheric moisture
overnight and releases it through evaporation during hot daytime hours, cooling the condenser
inlet air by 4–10 °C — with no pump, no external power, and no plumbing.

This repository holds the project website (`index.html`, `site-assets/`) and the Round 2 pitch
deck (`MEGHDOOT.pptx`).
